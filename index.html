Biography

I am an Associate Researcher at the School of Computer Science and Technology, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), affiliated with the State Key Laboratory of Cognitive Intelligence. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from USTC in 2023, supervised by Prof. Qi Liu. My research is conducted in collaboration with Prof. Enhong Chen and Prof. Qi Liu.

Research Interests

My research focuses on cognitive intelligence, with LLM agents and autonomous learning as general mechanisms and time series, scientific literature, and tools as core research objects. I study how intelligent systems reason, interact, and improve through experience.

  • LLMs and Agentic AI: Reinforcement learning, memory, and tool-augmented reasoning for autonomous agents that learn through interaction.
  • Time Series Mining: Context-aware forecasting and reasoning, with applications in electricity load, power generation, and weather prediction.
  • Scientific Literature Mining: Scientific retrieval, multimodal document understanding, knowledge extraction, and cross-paper reasoning grounded in evidence.

Applications: AI for Science (scientific literature and tool mining), Personalized Intelligence, and Forecasting Intelligence.
