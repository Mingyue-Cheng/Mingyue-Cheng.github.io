Research Overview

Cognitive Intelligence

My research focuses on cognitive intelligence: enabling machines to understand, reason, and interact. I study LLM agents and autonomous learning as general intelligent mechanisms, with scientific knowledge and tools and structured data as two core research objects. The three directions address independent scientific questions while sharing foundations in cognitive intelligence. Autonomous learning, continual evolution, and human–AI collaboration support long-term capability improvement.

Core Research Directions

01 · General Mechanisms of Intelligence

LLM Agents & Autonomous Learning

Study how LLM agents make autonomous decisions in open environments, accumulate experience, optimize learning strategies, and continually evolve. Research focuses on agent architectures and harnesses, long-term memory, autonomous interaction, and learning mechanisms.

Scientific question

How can LLM agents make autonomous decisions, accumulate transferable experience, and continually improve their capabilities?

Agent HarnessAgent MemorySelf-EvolutionAutonomous Learning

02 · Research for Scientific Innovation

Scientific Knowledge & Tool Mining

Discover, understand, and organize scientific knowledge and tools from research resources. Research spans scientific literature mining, knowledge extraction and reasoning, tool discovery, capability understanding, and autonomous tool composition to support scientific exploration and innovation.

Scientific question

How can scientific knowledge and tools be discovered, organized, and reliably used to support scientific innovation?

Scientific Literature MiningScientific Tool MiningKnowledge DiscoveryAgentic Research

03 · Research for Complex-System Cognition

Cognitive Intelligence for Structured Data

Study semantics, context, complex relationships, and dynamic evolution in time series, tables, and graphs, moving from pattern recognition toward knowledge-grounded cognitive reasoning, interactive analysis, and decision support for complex systems.

Scientific question

How can complex relationships and dynamic evolution in structured data be understood and reasoned about using knowledge and evidence?

Time SeriesTabular DataGraph Data
