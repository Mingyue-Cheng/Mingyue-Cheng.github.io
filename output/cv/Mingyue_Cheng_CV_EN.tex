\section{Research Profile}
My research centers on \textbf{LLM reasoning and AI agents}, with a focus on context-aware reasoning, autonomous interactive learning, continual adaptation, and agent evolution. Complex tasks in time-series intelligence and scientific intelligence provide the application settings for this research.

\textit{Core question: How can agents reliably solve problems through reasoning and interaction when information is incomplete, environments change, and feedback is costly?}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Time-series intelligence --- modeling the dynamic world.}
Context awareness, heterogeneous time-series modeling, slow-thinking cognitive reasoning, and autonomous agent interaction for understanding complex dynamic systems.
\item \textbf{Scientific intelligence --- understanding the scientific world.}
Scientific data, tools, literature and knowledge; tool-augmented scientific reasoning; and capability enhancement of scientific foundation models.
\item \textbf{Autonomous learning and evolving agents.}
Capability enhancement, agentic reinforcement learning, continual learning, memory, and the evolution of both models and agents through interaction.
\end{itemize}
