Foundations & Directions

Research

My research centers on large language models and agents, time-series data mining, and scientific literature mining. I pursue three connected directions: Forecasting Intelligence, AI for AI, and AI for Science, with a focus on predictive reasoning, autonomous learning, and scientific discovery.

Research Foundations

LLMs and Agentic AI

I study LLM reasoning and decision-making, agent planning and tool use, and agent harnesses for memory, context management, and reliable interaction.

Time Series Mining

I study time-series representation learning, forecasting, and cross-domain generalization, with a focus on transferable models for understanding temporal patterns and dependencies.

Scientific Literature Mining

I study scientific literature retrieval, multimodal understanding of text, figures, and tables, and the extraction and integration of knowledge and evidence across papers.

Research Directions

01 · Predictive Reasoning & Temporal Dynamics

Forecasting Intelligence

I study how models and agents use contextual evidence to understand and forecast dynamic systems. My research focuses on context-aware forecasting, evidence-driven predictive reasoning, and active context acquisition, with the long-term goal of understanding and discovering the mechanisms underlying temporal dynamics.

Selected work AlphaCast Time-R1

02 · Autonomous Learning & Continual Evolution

AI for AI

I study autonomous learning in large language models and continual evolution in agents through interaction, feedback, and accumulated experience. My research connects learning in model parameters with adaptation through memory and interaction, so models and agents can learn from experience and improve over time.

Selected work Agent-R1 MemWeaver

03 · AI-Ready Infrastructure & Autonomous Research

AI for Science

I study AI-ready scientific infrastructure and autonomous research agents, with a focus on making scientific data, literature, and tools usable by agents. My goal is to support iterative research through hypothesis generation, experiment planning and execution, and evidence-based validation, with human oversight.
