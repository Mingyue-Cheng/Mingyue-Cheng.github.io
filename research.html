Research Interests

My research centers on LLM-driven reasoning and AI agents, with a focus on context-aware reasoning, autonomous interactive, and continual learning and adaptation. This work is motivated by complex tasks in time-series intelligence and science intelligence (scientific knowledge and tool mining). Research Vision

How can agents reliably solve problems through reasoning and interaction when information is incomplete, environments change, and feedback is costly?

Prediction Intelligence

Building context-aware, reasoning-driven, and uncertainty-aware predictive intelligence for complex and evolving systems, enabling explainable forecasting and trustworthy decision support.
